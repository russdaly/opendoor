Why most church ads don't work

The ad was never the hard part.

Ads Manager is built for marketers. Most churches boost a post, see a few likes and stop.

Forms sit in an inbox for days

Someone finally raises their hand, and nobody texts back until Thursday. By then they've moved on.

Help costs more than the ads

An agency charges a monthly fee on top of ad spend, and still can't tell you who came on Sunday.

Questions

Things pastors ask us.

Do I need to know how to run Facebook ads?

No. open door writes the ad with your church's details, makes the picture with you, and sets up the form, campaign and ad in Facebook and Instagram. You never have to open Ads Manager.

How much should we spend on ads?

You choose, and it is separate from your open door plan. Many churches start at $10 to $20 a day, about $300 to $600 a month, paid straight to Meta from your own card. You can test with $5 a day. Ads are created paused, so nothing is spent until you press Turn on, and you can pause anytime. See what to expect.

Who actually texts the people who sign up?

Your team, with help. The first text goes out automatically, simple questions get automatic answers, and everything else waits for you with a reply already written. Nothing pastoral is ever answered automatically.

We already use Planning Center. Do we have to switch?

No. open door adds new guests to Planning Center for you and reads Sunday check-ins back, so each person's journey stays up to date.

How long does setup take?

About 10 minutes for the church details, since open door reads your website for you. Connecting Facebook is a few clicks. A new texting number needs carrier approval, which can take a few days.

Can we cancel?

Yes, anytime, from the Billing page. Your ads stay in your own Facebook account.
